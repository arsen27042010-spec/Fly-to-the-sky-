# 01 — Reference breakdown

Source: [`reference/reference-video.mp4`](../reference/reference-video.mp4) (20 s, vertical; caption "Thank god for the disguise"). Frames at 2 per second: [`reference/frames.jpg`](../reference/frames.jpg).

We take **the idea and the gameplay**, not the art or the level.

## Timeline

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | A rocket launches from a pad near the coast. Third-person camera behind it, blocky orange flame trail. It flies over green hills toward a grey house. A small "x" crosshair sits near the top of the screen. |
| 4–6 s | It descends and flies in through an open window. |
| 6–12 s | Indoors it moves in short bursts: engine on, a hop, then it stands upright on the floor. It works its way through corridors and doorways. |
| 12–18 s | A room with a tank and a TV. The rocket lands on a table and stands upright. Its paint (white body, green stems and leaves, yellow-white daisies) makes it look like a flower vase. The tank's turret turns straight toward it and does not notice. |
| 18–20 s | The turret turns away; the rocket hops onto the tank. The camera pulls back: hit. |

## What we take

- **Player = rocket**, third-person chase camera, crosshair aiming.
- **Hop-and-stand rhythm**: bursts of thrust, then standing upright on any flat surface.
- **Disguise**: standing still and upright is the hiding pose; the skin sells it as a household object.
- **A target that watches** (Sentinel): the tank both looks around and is the thing you must hit.
- **Strike when it looks away**: timing against a sweeping turret.
- **Blocky flame** made of squares: fits the stud style.

## What we change or add

- Inertia-driven flight with a full physics feel ([05-flight.md](05-flight.md)), not just hops.
- Jet wash: exhaust spreading over surfaces ([06-effects.md](06-effects.md)).
- A suspicion meter instead of instant detection ([07-guards-targets.md](07-guards-targets.md)).
- Progression, upgrades, skins, PRO servers.
- Our own world: a rocky alpine valley ([09-zone-1-rocks.md](09-zone-1-rocks.md)).
